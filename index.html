Inside the Book

Meet the Characters & Their Adventures

Every story features lovable animal friends on a journey that teaches a timeless lesson.

🐑
Story 1

Lily and the Lost Toy

Lily the lamb loses her beloved blue teddy bear. Her friend Pip the pig helps her search until they find it.

💚 True friends help each other when things are tough.
🐯
Story 2

Timmy's Sharing Surprise

Timmy the tiger has a basket of apples all to himself — until he realises that sharing feels even sweeter.

🍎 Sharing makes everyone happy.
🐿️
Story 3

The Brave Little Squirrel

During a fierce storm, tiny Squeaky risks everything to save two baby birds in a fallen nest.

⚡ Even the smallest can be the bravest.
🐇
Story 4

Rosie's Honest Mistake

Rosie's ball lands in Farmer McGregor's pumpkin patch. She faces a choice: sneak in, or be honest.

🎃 Honesty is always the best way.
🐻
Story 5

The Friendly Bear's Picnic

Barnaby the bear invites hungry squirrels to share his honey-and-berry picnic by the river.

🍯 Sharing brings joy to everyone.
🐒
Story 6

Max and the Helping Hand

Max the monkey stops his play to help Polly the parrot gather branches for her new nest.

🤝 Helping others makes tasks easier and hearts happier.
🐢
Story 7

The Kind Turtle's Gift

Slow Timmy the turtle guides a very thirsty Benny Bunny on a long walk to find a hidden spring.

💙 A kind heart always finds a way to help.
🐰
Story 8

The Respectful Rabbit

Barnaby rabbit is playing loudly near Mrs. Owl's home and learns the importance of being considerate.

🦉 Respecting others makes the world a better place.
🐛
Story 9

The Courageous Caterpillar

Carlos the caterpillar is afraid of heights but must climb a tall stem to transform into a butterfly.

🦋 Courage helps us overcome our fears.
🐦
Story 10

The Sharing Birds

Pip and Pop both find the same worm and nearly quarrel — until Mama Robin teaches them to share.

🪱 Sharing makes everyone happy and solves problems.
